# Brief: one poster, every size

A film client sends us one finished poster, the one sheet. Our designers then rebuild it by hand for every place it will run: social, out of home, display, in cinema. A campaign needs 20 to 40 sizes. Each takes a designer 15 to 40 minutes, and the legal lines (billing block, rating, release date) are where mistakes get expensive.

Build something that helps a designer get from the one sheet to the pack.

## You have

- `poster.png` the finished one sheet for *The Long Tide*
- `layers/` the same poster as separate layers, and `layers.json` saying where each sits, what it is, and the rule that goes with it
- `sizes.json` the sizes this campaign needs, with the areas each platform covers

## Notes

- Designers work in their design tool all day and do not want another window if they can help it.
- A designer must be able to change anything the tool proposes.
- Nothing can go to the client with a legal line missing, cropped or too small to read.
- Decide what problem to solve. A focused, working tool beats an ambitious unfinished one.
